What Johnson City homeowners should know about working with an AC contractor.

JUN 02, 2026 HOMEOWNER GUIDES 5 MIN READ

Plenty of Johnson City homeowners will work with an air conditioning contractor this year — and the difference between a quick, smooth job and a frustrating one that drags on for weeks usually comes down to how that relationship is handled on both sides. A little preparation goes a long way.

Six ways to get more from your contractor

  1. Keep a system log. Track filter changes, service dates, and any unusual sounds or smells. A good log helps a technician diagnose problems much faster.
  2. Schedule early. Book maintenance in spring, before the summer rush. You'll have far more flexibility on dates — and your system gets checked before it's needed most.
  3. Confirm the details up front. Before work begins, talk through the planned repair, the parts involved, and how long it should take.
  4. Stay reachable. Being available during the visit means quick decisions if the technician finds something unexpected — no stalled jobs.
  5. Ask for written summaries. A service report documenting what was inspected, found and completed is your system's medical chart. Keep them together.
  6. Ask about warranties and agreements. Get the terms of any maintenance plan or extended service agreement in writing, including what's covered and for how long.

Credentials worth verifying

Before anyone touches your system, it's fair — and smart — to check:

  • A current Tennessee contractor license (you can verify it yourself at verify.tn.gov)
  • EPA Section 608 certification for anyone handling refrigerant
  • Proof of liability insurance and workers' compensation
  • Manufacturer authorization — an authorized dealer installs to the standard the warranty requires
  • A written, itemized estimate before work starts
  • Written service agreement terms, not a handshake summary
  • Local references or third-party reviews from your area

A contractor who welcomes these questions is telling you something. One who dodges them is telling you something too.
