What to expect when your new air conditioner is installed.

MAR 04, 2026 INSTALLATION 4 MIN READ

A new air conditioner is only as good as its installation. Done right, it means efficient cooling, long-term system health and lower utility bills. Done poorly, it means reduced comfort — and often a voided manufacturer warranty. Here's how the process should look, start to finish.

Before the appointment

  • Clear the work areas, indoors and out
  • Secure pets and keep children clear of the work zones
  • Mention any concerns or quirks of the house to the technicians up front
  • Make sure there's access to the electrical panel and any crawlspaces

On installation day

  1. Walkthrough and preparation. The crew confirms the plan, protects floors and surfaces, and stages the equipment.
  2. Removal of the old system. Existing equipment is disconnected and removed safely — including proper refrigerant recovery.
  3. The new installation. Outdoor condenser, refrigerant lines, indoor components and thermostat, connected and sealed to spec.
  4. Testing and commissioning. The system is run, measured and tuned before the crew considers the job done.

After the install

Expect a demonstration of your new system and thermostat, a full cleanup, plain-English maintenance guidance, and your warranty documentation — registered and explained, not just left on the counter.

The quality markers

Transparent communication. Punctual arrival. Explanations you can actually follow. A demonstration before they leave, and advice on getting the best performance from day one. If your installer treats those as standard, you chose well.
