Five signs your air conditioner is asking for help.

MAR 31, 2026 REPAIR 4 MIN READ

A Johnson City summer doesn't forgive a failing air conditioner. The frustrating part is that most breakdowns don't come out of nowhere — small, ignorable issues escalate quietly until the hottest week of the year finds them. Catching the early warnings prevents discomfort, extends your system's lifespan, improves efficiency, and keeps your indoor air healthy.

1. Weak airflow

Clogged filters, blocked ductwork or a failing blower motor all choke airflow. The system compensates by working harder — which raises energy use, strains components, and can end in motor burnout or compressor failure if left alone.

2. Warm or inconsistent air

If vents blow warm, or one room is an icebox while another swelters, suspect low refrigerant, a struggling compressor, or a failing expansion valve. These are exactly the symptoms that precede a complete failure during peak heat.

3. Unusual noises

Grinding, squealing, rattling or buzzing is mechanical or electrical trouble announcing itself — typically a failing motor or a worn component. The sooner a professional hears it, the cheaper the fix tends to be.

4. Short cycling

An AC that switches on and off in rapid bursts is wasting energy and wearing itself out. The usual culprits: thermostat problems, a refrigerant imbalance, or a unit that was never sized correctly for the space.

5. Rising energy bills

A bill that climbs with no change in habits is the quietest warning sign of all. Clogged filters, refrigerant leaks and worn parts all force the system to run longer and harder for the same result — and you pay the difference every month.

Why acting fast pays

  • Small repairs stay small — and cheap
  • Your home stays comfortable through the season
  • Efficiency recovers, and bills come back down
  • The system lasts years longer
  • Air quality improves
  • You skip the 2 a.m. emergency breakdown entirely
